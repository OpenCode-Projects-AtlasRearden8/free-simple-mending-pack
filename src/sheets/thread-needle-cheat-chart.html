Thread and Needle Cheat Chart

Print once, tape it inside your sewing basket. It answers the only two questions that matter at the start of a mend: which thread, and which needle.

Which thread should I use?

You're fixingThread to pickWhy
Everyday shirt or pants buttonsAll-purpose polyester, doubledStrong and stretchy; won't break when the button flexes
Coat button or heavy jacketHeavy-duty or doubled all-purpose, quadrupledCoat buttons take real strain
Jeans, denim, workwearHeavy-duty polyester or topstitch threadSurvives friction and repeated washing
Knits and sweatersMatching yarn or fine wool threadMoves with the fabric; polyester thread feels stiff
Delicates, silk, liningFine cotton or silk thread, single strandToo-thick thread bulks up fine fabric
Zippers, bags, repairs under strainHeavy-duty polyesterWeak thread is why zips keep failing

Which needle should I use?

FabricNeedleRemember
Woven (shirts, denim, linen)Sharp needle, size 9–11Small needle, small hole, invisible mends
Knits, sweaters, JerseyBlunt tapestry or ballpoint needleBlunt tips slide between the yarns instead of piercing them
Heavy layers (hems, coats)Mid-size sharp, size 7–9Poke through with a thimble, not shoulder strength
Beads, buttons on heavy fabricMid-size sharpPass the button three or four times and stop there
Silk and fine fabricsThe finest needle you haveFine needle leaves no mark behind

Quick translator

What you'll hearPlain meaning
Sharps vs. tapestry needlesPointed tip vs. rounded tip. Pointed for woven fabric, rounded for knits.
Needle “sizes” (5, 7, 9, 11)Higher number = finer needle. Start at 9 for cloth work; 3–5 for tough jobs.
“Single” vs. “doubled” threadOne strand through the needle vs. two, tied together. Doubled is faster and stronger.
Thread “weight” (30, 40, 50)Higher = thinner thread. Heavy-duty threads are the low numbers.
Slipstitch, whipstitch, running stitchHiding stitches in a fold vs. looping around an edge vs. a simple straight ladder.
Kind tips: If you're between options, the all-purpose thread and a medium sharp needle will get 90 percent of home mends done. You could buy an entire wardrobe of needles and never outgrow that one.
